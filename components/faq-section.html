Looking for Answers?

Find answers to common questions about gynecological care, treatments and health management.

Regular visits to a gynaecologist help monitor reproductive health, identify problems early, and provide appropriate preventive and treatment care.

Choosing a gynaecologist depends on experience, specialization, patient care, treatment options, and your individual healthcare requirements.

Look for appropriate qualifications, clinical experience, communication, patient-centered care, and expertise relevant to your condition.

Infertility can have several causes, including ovulation disorders, fallopian tube problems, endometriosis, age-related fertility changes, and other reproductive conditions.

Contraceptive choices include barrier methods, hormonal methods, intrauterine devices, and permanent options. The most suitable choice depends on individual health and family-planning goals.

Endometriosis occurs when tissue similar to the uterine lining grows outside the uterus. Evaluation may include symptoms, examination, imaging, and sometimes minimally invasive procedures.

Some gynecological conditions can affect fertility. Early evaluation and appropriate treatment can help identify possible causes and available options.
